So instead of asking for your trust, I made everything verifiable. This page answers who built this, what it discloses, and what it does not do.

  • Operator — jsontype (developer)

  • What we do — We build free web apps that run right in your browser, with no installation or sign-up. We currently run the crypto buy-timing signal and VOCA DECK (vocabulary flashcards).

I am a developer, not a financial professional. I will not hide that. This dashboard is not an investment advisory service; it is a tool I built myself to view public indicators on a single screen against a consistent standard. That is why the basis for trust is not “who I am” but “what I disclose.”

Why you can trust it — what we disclose

Most “buy signal” sites show you only the score and hide the formula. I do the opposite.

1. Every formula is public

The score methodology document spells out everything — the normalization functions that map the 6 indicators onto 0–100, the actual figures of the two weight sets, short-term and long-term, how weights are renormalized when an indicator is missing, and even the cut-off values of the verdict bands.

Short-term (momentum) RSI 28% · Fear & Greed 28% · Drawdown 18% · MACD 14% · Cross 8% · Mayer 4%
Long-term (contrarian) RSI 18% · MACD 18% · Fear & Greed 18% · Mayer 18% · Drawdown 14% · Cross 14%

A black box cannot be verified, and what cannot be verified cannot be trusted. If you don’t like the numbers, check them yourself. That is exactly why they are public.

2. Limitations are not hidden

Articles that sell indicators only show the cases where the indicator worked. I write separately, for each indicator, about “when it betrays you.”

  • RSI can stay wrong for weeks at a time in a strongly trending market — there really are phases where “overbought, so sell” is the worst possible move
  • A fear reading shows up both at bottoms and in the middle of a crash — low does not mean bottom
  • The Mayer Multiple has almost no short-term predictive power — which is why its short-term weight is minimized to 4%
  • A drawdown has no floor — buy at −50% and it can still go to −80%
  • This model knows nothing about fundamentals, news or regulation

For details, see the in-depth indicator guide and the limitations section of the methodology.

I disclose this openly. Knowing how this site makes money is the reader’s right.

  • The source of revenue is Google AdSense advertising. That is all.
  • There are no exchange affiliate links. Wherever you trade, I don’t receive a single cent.
  • I do not recommend specific coins or accept sponsorship for them. The coins on the dashboard were selected by market cap and trading volume, and none was added in exchange for payment.
  • I do not run paid signals, “trading rooms” or Telegram channels.
  • The score is calculated only from public data and a public formula. I do not tweak it by hand.
Ad revenue does not change no matter what you buy. So I have no incentive to tell you to “buy now.” In fact, during overheated phases this site displays OVERHEATED (caution on new buys) just as it is.

Editorial principles

  1. Formulas are made public. If a model has to be hidden, I don’t use it in the first place.
  2. Limitations come first. No article is published without stating the phases in which the indicator fails.
  3. No investment advice. I never say “buy” or “sell” — only “where the current phase is.”
  4. Sources are credited. I never present someone else’s data as my own.
  5. If something is uncertain, I say it is uncertain. Backtest results are only the past, and are labeled as such.

Reporting errors and corrections

If an indicator is calculated incorrectly, an explanation contains a factual error, or the data looks odd, please let me know. Send it to support@broodev.com and I will check and fix it; if the correction affects the content, I will note the fact of the correction and its date on that page.

Every content page shows a “Last updated” date at the top.

What this site does not do

  • ❌ Predict future prices — this score is not a forecast. It is a summary of the current phase
  • ❌ Give investment advice or solicit purchases — I am not qualified to, and I don’t
  • ❌ Guarantee returns — no indicator protects you from losses
  • ❌ Collect personal data — there is no sign-up, and settings are stored only in your browser (localStorage) (Privacy Policy)
