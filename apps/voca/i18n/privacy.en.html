Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-07-03 · Operator: Y-Systems (the “Operator”) · Service: broodev.com (VOCA DECK)

The Operator values users’ personal information and complies with applicable laws and regulations. This policy applies to the Flashing Memorizer vocabulary deck service (VOCA DECK) provided at broodev.com.

1. Personal information collected and how it is collected

This service has no member registration, and you are never asked to enter personal information such as your name or contact details in order to use it. Information is processed only in the following cases.

  • When you contact us: if you contact us by email (support@broodev.com), your email address and the content of your inquiry are processed in order to reply.
  • Automatic collection: standard log information such as browser, device and cookie data may be generated while you use the service (see Section 3 below).
  • Local storage: the decks you open (words and meanings), your “memorized” marks and your display settings (font, background, playback settings) are stored only in your browser’s localStorage and are never sent or uploaded to a server. They are deleted when you delete your browser data.

2. Purposes of use

  • Providing and maintaining the service, and responding to user inquiries
  • Improving the service and analyzing usage statistics
  • Displaying ads and preventing fraudulent use

3. Cookies and advertising (Google AdSense)

This service displays third-party ads such as Google AdSense, and cookies may be used in the process.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user’s prior visits.
  • Google’s use of advertising cookies (such as DoubleClick/DART cookies) enables Google and its partners to show personalized ads based on users’ visits to this site and other sites.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising in Google Ads Settings, and opt out of third-party vendors’ cookies all at once at www.aboutads.info.
  • Most browsers let you block or delete cookies in their settings, but some features may be limited if you do so.

For details, see Google’s advertising policies.

4. Disclosure to third parties

The Operator does not sell or provide users’ personal information to third parties. The advertising cookies described in Section 3 above are governed by the policies of each advertising provider. TTS (text-to-speech) uses the browser’s built-in speech synthesis and does not send text externally (subject to the browser’s implementation).

5. Retention and destruction

Inquiry emails are destroyed without delay once the purpose of processing has been fulfilled. Automatically collected logs are retained only to the extent necessary for statistics and security. Deck data is not stored on a server, so there is nothing for the Operator to retain or destroy.

6. Rights of users

Users may request access to, correction of or deletion of their personal information; requests are accepted at the contact address below. Users can delete their deck data themselves in their browser.

7. Privacy officer and contact

Operator: Y-Systems · Email: support@broodev.com

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be revised in response to changes in laws or in the service. Any revision will be posted on this page together with its effective date.
