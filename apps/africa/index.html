What is Africa Daily Utility?

A free, data-light utility panel for daily life in Nigeria, Kenya, Ghana and South Africa. It puts four things on one fast page: power outage information (South African load-shedding stages explained, with EskomSePush, Eskom, KPLC and ECG official links), money tools — live mid-market exchange rates for NGN, KES, GHS and ZAR, a currency converter and a remittance true-cost checker that reveals the hidden exchange-rate margin on top of the fee (Sub-Saharan remittances average ~8%, the world’s most expensive corridor), official status portals — JAMB results, WAEC result checkers, NIN services, Nigerian passports, SASSA SRD grant status, matric results, KNEC and eCitizen — and verified USSD codes, including Nigeria’s NCC-harmonised codes (*310# balance, *312# data, *996# NIN-SIM) and bank codes (*737#, *901#, *919#, *966#, *894#). No app install, no account — it works on cheap data and old Android phones.
