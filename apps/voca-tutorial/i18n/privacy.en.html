Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-07-03 · Operator: Y-Systems (the “Operator”) · Service: voca-tutorial.broodev.com (VOCA_DECK)

The Operator takes users’ personal information seriously and complies with applicable laws. This Policy applies to the Flashing Memorizer vocabulary deck tutorial service (VOCA_TUTORIAL) provided at voca-tutorial.broodev.com.

1. Personal Information Collected and How

This Service has no account registration and does not ask you to enter personal information such as your name or contact details in order to use it. Information is processed only in the following cases.

  • When you contact us: If you contact us by email (support@broodev.com), your email address and the content of your inquiry are processed in order to reply.
  • Automatic collection: Standard log information such as browser, device and cookie data may be generated while you use the Service (see Section 3 below).
  • Local storage: The decks you open (words and meanings), your “memorized” marks and your display settings (font, background, playback settings) are stored only in your browser’s localStorage and are never sent or uploaded to a server. They are deleted when you clear your browser data.

2. Purposes of Use

  • Displaying advertisements and preventing fraudulent use

For details, please see Google’s advertising policies.

4. Disclosure to Third Parties

The Operator does not sell or provide users’ personal information to third parties. The advertising cookies described in Section 3 above are governed by each advertising provider’s policies. Inquiry emails are destroyed without delay once the purpose of processing has been fulfilled. Automatically collected logs are retained only to the extent necessary for statistical and security purposes. Because deck data is not stored on any server, there is nothing for the Operator to retain or destroy.
