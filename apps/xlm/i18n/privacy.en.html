1. Personal Information Collected and How

The Service has no account registration and does not ask users to enter personal information such as names or contact details in order to use it. Information is processed only in the following cases.

  • When you contact us: If you contact us by email (support@broodev.com), your email address and the content of your inquiry are processed in order to respond.

For details, please see Google’s advertising policies.
